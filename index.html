My Skills

In my project, I have created several 3D animations using different modeling and animation techniques. Some of the main projects I worked on include a medieval sword and a NASCAR 3D car. I created the models, added materials and textures, and then animated them to make the objects look more realistic and interesting.

For the medieval sword, I focused on creating the shape and details of the sword and making the animation look smooth. For the NASCAR 3D project, I worked on creating a racing car and used animation and camera movements to give it a more dynamic look. Through these projects, I learned more about 3D modeling, texturing, lighting, animation, and rendering. These projects also helped me improve my creativity and understand how 3D objects can be designed and animated.
